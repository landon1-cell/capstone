Pros and Cons of AI

In the modern post-covid years, lots of things have affected us as a society. Ever since about 2023, one of those has been AI or artificial intelligence. Artificial intelligence has been a very touchy subject. People either hate or love AI. I want to explore and provide all the pros and cons of AI and why it is a topic that divides us so much.
Initially a lot of us viewed AI very positively. It was made by us for us to help us with things. One of these things can be research and peer reviewing, which is a very tedious task for us writing papers and doing research for them. As said by the national library of medicine:“The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) has transformed various aspects of scientific research, including academic publishing and peer review. In recent years, AI tools such as large language models have demonstrated their capability to streamline numerous tasks traditionally handled by human editors and reviewers.”
So AI is doing a good job at peer reviewing and more however there are many problems that come with AI helping us. “While AI substantially benefits the efficiency and accuracy of academic processes, its integration raises critical ethical and methodological questions, particularly in peer review. AI lacks the subtle understanding of complex scientific content that human expertise provides, posing challenges in evaluating research novelty and significance.” The inaccuracies from AI have become a glaring problem to everyone using it. This renders AI seemingly useless. This isn’t the only con AI carries.
In recent years, another problem with the world has been climate change and keeping the world healthy. Unfortunately, AI isn’t helping the situation. Massachusetts institutes of technology estimates AI consumes four or five times the magnitude of energy from a typical search engine request. Also telling us that cars in their whole life time amit only ⅕ of the amount of carbon admissions an AI model does. This renders AI as a serious harm to the environment.
Although AI can also help in more ways than one might think, generating images and more. For those in workplaces they might use it to bump up their own creativity. “One of the most promising aspects of generative AI is its ability to democratize creativity. In the workplace, employees who may not consider themselves creative can use AI to generate ideas, visualize prototypes, or write compelling content. This levels the playing field and encourages broader participation in innovation.” While AI can help with research and innovation, AI’s contribution to worsening global health is what makes it such a touchy topic.
